Veni Sancte Spíritus, | Come, Holy Spirit,
Et emítte cǽlitus | and send forth from heaven
Lucis tuæ rádium. | the ray of Thy light.
Veni pater páuperum, | Come, Father of the poor,
Veni dator múnerum, | come, Giver of gifts,
Veni lumen córdium. | come, Light of hearts.
Consolátor óptime, Dulcis hospes ánimæ, | Best of comforters, sweet Guest of the soul,
Dulce refrigérium. | sweet refreshment.
In labóre réquies, In æstu tempéries, | In labour, rest; in heat, coolness;
In fletu solátium. | in weeping, solace.
O lux beatíssima, Reple cordis íntima | O most blessed Light, fill the inmost heart
Tuórum fidélium. | of Thy faithful.
Sine tuo númine, | Without Thy divine power
Nihil est in hómine, | there is nothing in man,
Nihil est innóxium. | nothing that is without harm.
Lava quod est sórdidum, | Wash what is soiled,
Riga quod est áridum, | water what is dry,
Sana quod est sáucium. | heal what is wounded.
Flecte quod est rígidum, Fove quod est frígidum, | Bend what is stiff, warm what is cold,
Rege quod est dévium. | guide what has strayed.
Da tuis fidélibus, In te confidéntibus, | Give to Thy faithful, who trust in Thee,
Sacrum septenárium. | the sacred sevenfold gift.
Da virtútis méritum, Da salútis éxitum, | Give the reward of virtue, give a saving end,
Da perénne gáudium. | give everlasting joy.
Amen. Allelúia. | Amen. Alleluia.

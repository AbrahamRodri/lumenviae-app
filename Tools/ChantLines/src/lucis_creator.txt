# Lucis Creator optime (Mode VIII), the Sunday hymn at Vespers, all five
# stanzas and the Amen. Latin from the score; English a plain, literal
# rendering. The cantor breathes at every quarter of a stanza, so a line
# is a quarter.

Lucis Creátor óptime, | O most kind Creator of the light,
Lucem diérum próferens, | who bringest forth the light of days,
Primórdiis lucis novæ | and with the first beginnings of new light
Mundi parans oríginem: | preparest the origin of the world:
Qui mane junctum vésperi | Thou who biddest morning joined to evening
Diem vocári prǽcipis: | to be called day,
Illábitur tetrum chaos, | now dreadful darkness falls upon us;
Audi preces cum flétibus. | hear our prayers and our tears.
Ne mens graváta crímine, | Lest the mind, weighed down by sin,
Vitæ sit exsul múnere, | be an exile from the gift of life,
Dum nil perénne cógitat, | while it thinks on nothing everlasting
Seséque culpis ílligat. | and binds itself in its faults.
Cæléste pulset óstium, | May it knock at the door of heaven,
Vitále tollat prǽmium: | may it win the prize of life;
Vitémus omne nóxium, | let us shun all that harms,
Purgémus omne péssimum. | let us cleanse away all that is evil.
Præsta, Pater piíssime, | Grant this, most loving Father,
Patríque compar Únice, | and Thou, the Only-begotten, equal to the Father,
Cum Spíritu Paráclito, | with the Spirit, the Paraclete,
Regnans per omne sǽculum. | reigning through every age.
Amen. | Amen.

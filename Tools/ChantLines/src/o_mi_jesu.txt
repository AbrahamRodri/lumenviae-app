# Fatima Prayer and the Prayer Book's O Mary, Conceived without Sin.

O mi Jesu, dimítte nobis, | O my Jesus, forgive us our sins,
líbera nos ab igne inférni; | save us from the fires of hell,
perduc in cælum omnes ánimas, præsértim illas quæ máxime índigent misericórdiæ tuæ. | and lead all souls to heaven, especially those in most need of Thy mercy.
O María sine labe concépta. | O Mary, conceived without sin,
Ora pro nobis qui confúgimus ad te. | pray for us who have recourse to thee.

O salutáris Hóstia, | O saving Victim,
Quæ cæli pandis óstium, Bella premunt hostília, | Who openest the gate of heaven, hostile wars press hard upon us:
Da robur, fer auxílium. | give strength, bring aid.
Uni trinóque Dómino | To the Lord, One and Three,
Sit sempitérna glória, Qui vitam sine término | be glory everlasting; Who life without an end
Nobis donet in pátria. | may grant us in our homeland.
Amen. | Amen.

# Jesu Redemptor omnium, the Vespers hymn of Christmas: all seven stanzas,
# a breath at every line, and the Amen. Latin from the score; English a
# plain literal rendering (no app prayer).
noise -16
gap 0.3
merge 1.5

Jesu Redémptor ómnium, | Jesus, Redeemer of all,
Quem lucis ante oríginem, | Whom, before the beginning of light,
Parem patérnæ glóriæ, | equal to the Father's glory,
Pater suprémus édidit. | the Father most high begot.
Tu lumen et splendor Patris, | Thou light and splendour of the Father,
Tu spes perénnis ómnium: | Thou everlasting hope of all,
Inténde quas fundunt preces | give ear to the prayers poured forth
Tui per orbem sérvuli. | by Thy servants throughout the world.
Meménto, rerum Cónditor, | Remember, O Maker of all things,
Nostri quod olim córporis, | that once, of our body,
Sacráta ab alvo Vírginis | from the hallowed womb of the Virgin,
Nascéndo, formam súmpseris. | being born, Thou didst take the form.
Testátur hoc præsens dies, | This present day bears witness,
Currens per anni círculum, | running its course through the circle of the year,
Quod solus e sinu Patris | that Thou alone, from the bosom of the Father,
Mundi salus advéneris. | didst come, the salvation of the world.
Hunc astra, tellus, ǽquora, | Him the stars, the earth, the seas,
Hunc omne quod cælo subest, | Him all that is beneath the heavens,
Salútis auctórem novæ | the Author of a new salvation,
Novo salútat cántico. | greet with a new song.
Et nos, beáta quos sacri | And we, whom the blessed stream
Rigávit unda sánguinis, | of sacred Blood hath watered,
Natális ob diem tui, | for the day of Thy birth
Hymni tribútum sólvimus. | pay the tribute of a hymn.
Jesu, tibi sit glória, | Jesus, to Thee be glory,
Qui natus es de Vírgine, | Who wast born of the Virgin,
Cum Patre et almo Spíritu, | with the Father and the loving Spirit,
In sempitérna sǽcula. | unto everlasting ages.
Amen. | Amen.

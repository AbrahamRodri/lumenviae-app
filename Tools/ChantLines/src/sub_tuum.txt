Sub tuum præsídium confúgimus, | We fly to thy patronage,
sancta Dei Génitrix: | O holy Mother of God;
nostras deprecatiónes ne despícias in necessitátibus: | despise not our petitions in our necessities,
sed a perículis cunctis líbera nos semper, Virgo gloriósa et benedícta. | but deliver us always from all dangers, O glorious and blessed Virgin.
Ora pro nobis, sancta Dei Génitrix. | Pray for us, O holy Mother of God.
Ut digni efficiámur promissiónibus Christi. | That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.

a præsénti liberári tristítia, et ætérna pérfrui lætítia. | may be delivered from present sadness, and enter into the joy of Thine eternal gladness.

# Te saeculorum Principem (Mode I), the hymn of Christ the King, all eight
# stanzas and the Amen. Latin from the score; English a plain, literal
# rendering. The cantor breathes at every quarter of a stanza, so a line
# is a quarter.
noise -18
gap 0.25
merge 2.5

Te sæculórum Príncipem, | Thee, the Prince of the ages,
Te, Christe, Regem Géntium, | Thee, O Christ, the King of the nations,
Te méntium, te córdium | Thee, of minds and of hearts,
Unum fatémur árbitrum. | we confess the one Judge.
Scelésta turba clámitat: | The wicked throng cries out:
Regnáre Christum nólumus: | We will not have Christ to reign;
Te nos ovántes ómnium | we with rejoicing call Thee
Regem suprémum dícimus. | the supreme King of all.
O Christe, Princeps Pácifer, | O Christ, Prince who bringest peace,
Mentes rebélles súbjice: | subdue rebellious minds,
Tuóque amóre dévios, | and by Thy love those gone astray
Ovíle in unum cóngrega. | gather into one fold.
Ad hoc cruénta ab árbore, | For this, from the blood-stained tree,
Pendes apértis bráchiis: | Thou hangest with arms outstretched,
Diráque fossum cúspide | and, pierced by the cruel spear,
Cor igne flagrans éxhibes. | showest Thy Heart burning with fire.
Ad hoc in aris ábderis | For this Thou art hidden on the altars
Vini dapísque imágine, | under the likeness of wine and bread,
Fundens salútem fíliis | pouring out salvation for Thy children
Transverberáto péctore. | from Thy pierced breast.
Te natiónum Prǽsides | May the rulers of the nations
Honóre tollant público, | exalt Thee with public honour;
Colant magístri, júdices, | may teachers and judges revere Thee,
Leges et artes éxprimant. | may laws and arts set Thee forth.
Submíssa regum fúlgeant | May the humbled insignia of kings
Tibi dicáta insígnia: | shine as dedicated to Thee;
Mitíque sceptro pátriam | and bring under Thy gentle sceptre
Domósque subde cívium. | our country and the homes of its people.
Jesu, tibi sit glória, | Jesus, glory be to Thee,
Qui sceptra mundi témperas, | who rulest the sceptres of the world,
Cum Patre, et almo Spíritu, | with the Father and the loving Spirit,
In sempitérna sǽcula. | unto everlasting ages.
Amen. | Amen.

Jesu nostra redémptio, | O Jesus, our redemption,
Amor et desidérium, | our love and our desire,
Deus Creátor ómnium, | God, Creator of all things,
Homo in fine témporum. | made Man at the end of the ages.
Quæ te vicit cleméntia, | What mercy overcame Thee,
Ut ferres nostra crímina, | that Thou shouldst bear our sins,
Crudélem mortem pátiens, | suffering a cruel death,
Ut nos a morte tólleres! | to take us up from death!
Inférni claustra pénetrans, | Breaking through the gates of hell,
Tuos captívos rédimens: | redeeming Thine own captives,
Victor triúmpho nóbili, | Victor in noble triumph,
Ad dextram Patris résidens. | Thou art seated at the Father's right hand.
Ipsa te cogat píetas, | May Thy own loving kindness move Thee
Ut mala nostra súperes | to overcome our evils
Parcéndo, et voti cómpotes | by sparing us, and, granting our prayer,
Nos tuo vultu sáties. | to fill us with the sight of Thy face.
Tu esto nostrum gáudium, | Be Thou our joy,
Qui es futúrus prǽmium: | who art to be our reward;
Sit nostra in te glória | may our glory be in Thee
Per cuncta semper sǽcula. | through all ages for ever.
Amen. | Amen.

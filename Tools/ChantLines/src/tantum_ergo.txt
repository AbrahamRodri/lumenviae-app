Tantum ergo Sacraméntum | So great a Sacrament, therefore,
Venerémur cérnui: | let us venerate, bowing low;
Et antíquum documéntum | and let the former teaching
Novo cedat rítui: | give way to the new rite;
Præstet fides suppleméntum | let faith provide its aid
Sénsuum deféctui. | for the failing of the senses.
Genitóri, Genitóque | To the Begetter and the Begotten
Laus et jubilátio, | be praise and jubilation,
Salus, honor, virtus quoque | salvation, honour, and might also,
Sit et benedíctio: | and blessing be to Them;
Procedénti ab utróque | to Him Who proceedeth from Both
Compar sit laudátio. | be equal praise.
Amen. | Amen.
Panem de cælo præstitísti eis. | Thou didst give them bread from heaven.
Omne delectaméntum in se habéntem. | Containing in itself all delight.
Orémus. | Let us pray.
Deus, qui nobis sub Sacraménto mirábili passiónis tuæ memóriam reliquísti: | O God, Who under a wonderful Sacrament hast left us a memorial of Thy Passion,
tríbue, quǽsumus, | grant us, we beseech Thee,
ita nos córporis et sánguinis tui sacra mystéria venerári; | so to venerate the sacred mysteries of Thy Body and Blood,
ut redemptiónis tuæ fructum in nobis júgiter sentiámus. | that we may continually feel within ourselves the fruit of Thy redemption.
Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. | Who livest and reignest world without end.
Amen. | Amen.

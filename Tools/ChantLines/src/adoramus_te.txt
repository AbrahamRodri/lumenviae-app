# Adoramus te, sanctissime Domine Jesu Christe (Mode I). Latin from the
# score; English plain and literal. The cantor breathes at the bar inside
# the melisma of tibi, which the text cannot part, so that line runs over
# both phrases.

Adorámus te, | We adore Thee,
sanctíssime Dómine Jesu Christe, | most holy Lord Jesus Christ,
hic et ad omnes ecclésias tuas | here and in all Thy churches
quæ sunt in toto mundo, | which are throughout the whole world,
et benedícimus tibi, | and we bless Thee,
~
quia per sanctam crucem tuam redemísti mundum. | because by Thy holy Cross Thou hast redeemed the world.

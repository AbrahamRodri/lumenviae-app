Víctimæ pascháli laudes | Praises to the Paschal Victim
ímmolent Christiáni. | let Christians offer.
Agnus redémit oves: | The Lamb hath redeemed the sheep;
Christus ínnocens Patri | Christ, the innocent, unto the Father
reconciliávit | hath reconciled
peccatóres. | sinners.
Mors et vita duéllo conflixére mirándo: | Death and life have contended in a wondrous combat;
dux vitæ mórtuus, | the Prince of life, who died,
regnat vivus. | reigns alive.
Dic nobis María, quid vidísti | Tell us, Mary, what didst thou see
in via? | on the way?
Sepúlcrum Christi vivéntis, | The tomb of the living Christ,
et glóriam vidi resurgéntis: | and I saw the glory of His rising;
Angélicos testes, sudárium, et vestes. | the angelic witnesses, the napkin, and the garments.
Surréxit Christus spes mea: | Christ my hope is risen;
præcédet suos in Galilǽam. | He will go before His own into Galilee.
Scimus Christum surrexísse | We know that Christ is risen
a mórtuis vere: | truly from the dead;
tu nobis, victor Rex, | on us, O victorious King,
miserére. | have mercy.
Amen. Allelúia. | Amen. Alleluia.

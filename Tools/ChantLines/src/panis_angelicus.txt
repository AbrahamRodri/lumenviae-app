Panis angélicus | The bread of Angels
fit panis hóminum; | becomes the bread of men;
Dat panis cǽlicus | the bread of heaven
figúris términum: | puts an end to figures.
O res mirábilis! | O wondrous thing!
mandúcat Dóminum | there feeds upon the Lord
Pauper, servus, et húmilis. | the poor man, the servant, and the lowly.
Te trina Déitas | Thee, Godhead three
únaque póscimus: | and one, we beseech:
Sic nos tu vísita, | so do Thou visit us
sicut te cólimus; | as we worship Thee;
Per tuas sémitas | by Thy paths
duc nos quo téndimus, | lead us whither we are bound,
Ad lucem quam inhábitas. | to the light wherein Thou dwellest.
Amen. | Amen.

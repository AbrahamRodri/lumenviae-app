# Salve festa dies, the Easter processional of Venantius Fortunatus
# (Mode IV): the refrain sung twice, then after each of the seven verses.
# The cantor sings the refrain's halves now in one breath, now in two,
# and its last two pieces once in a breath; those pieces stand together
# as one line. Latin from the score; English plain and literal.

Salve festa dies, | Hail, festal day,
toto venerábilis ævo, Qua Deus inférnum vicit et astra tenet. | to be honoured through every age, on which God conquered hell and holds the stars.
Salve festa dies, toto venerábilis ævo, | Hail, festal day, to be honoured through every age,
Qua Deus inférnum vicit et astra tenet. | on which God conquered hell and holds the stars.
Ecce renascéntis testátur grátia mundi | Behold, the grace of the world reborn bears witness
Omnia cum Dómino dona redísse suo. | that all gifts have returned with their Lord.
Salve festa dies, toto venerábilis ævo, | Hail, festal day, to be honoured through every age,
Qua Deus inférnum vicit et astra tenet. | on which God conquered hell and holds the stars.
Namque triumphánti post trístia tártara Christo | For to Christ, triumphant after the gloom of hell,
Úndique fronde nemus, | the grove on every side with leaf,
grámina flore favent. | the grasses with flower, give their welcome.

Tértia lux rédiit, surge sepúlte meus. | the third day has returned; arise, my Buried One.

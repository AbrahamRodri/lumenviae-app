O gloriósa Vírginum | O glorious one among virgins,
Sublímis inter sídera: | lofty among the stars,
Qui te creávit, párvulum | Him who created thee, as a little Child,
Lacténte nutris úbere. | thou dost nourish at thy breast.
Quod Heva tristis ábstulit, | What sorrowful Eve took away,
Tu reddis almo gérmine: | thou restorest through thy gracious Offspring;
Intrent ut astra flébiles, | that those who weep may enter the stars,
Cæli reclúdis cárdines. | thou openest the doors of heaven.
Tu Regis alti jánua, | Thou art the gate of the King on high,
Et aula lucis fúlgida: | and the shining hall of light:
Vitam datam per Vírginem, | for the life given through the Virgin,
Gentes redémptæ, pláudite. | ye redeemed nations, give praise.
Jesu, tibi sit glória, | Jesus, to Thee be glory,
Qui natus es de Vírgine, | Who wast born of the Virgin,
Cum Patre et almo Spíritu, | with the Father and the gracious Spirit,
In sempitérna sǽcula. | for everlasting ages.
Amen. | Amen.

Quem terra, pontus, sídera | Him whom earth and sea and stars
Colunt, adórant, prǽdicant, | worship, adore and proclaim,
Trinam regéntem máchinam, | who rules the threefold frame of the world,
Claustrum Maríæ bájulat. | the cloister of Mary carries.
Cui luna, sol et ómnia | He whom moon and sun and all things
Desérviunt per témpora, | serve through every age,
Perfúsa cæli grátia, | filled with the grace of heaven,
Gestant puéllæ víscera. | the maiden's womb now bears.
Beáta Mater múnere, | Blessed Mother by this gift,
Cujus, supérnus Ártifex | whose Maker most high,
Mundum pugíllo cóntinens, | holding the world in His hand,
Ventris sub arca clausus est. | was enclosed within the ark of her womb.
Beáta cæli núntio, | Blessed by the message of heaven,
Fecúnda Sancto Spíritu, | fruitful by the Holy Spirit,
Desiderátus géntibus | the Desired of the nations
Cujus per alvum fusus est. | was brought forth from her womb.
Jesu, tibi sit glória, | Glory be to Thee, O Jesus,
Qui natus es de Vírgine, | who art born of the Virgin,
Cum Patre et almo Spíritu, | with the Father and the loving Spirit,
In sempitérna sǽcula. | unto everlasting ages.
Amen. | Amen.

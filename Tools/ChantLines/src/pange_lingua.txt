part 0
Pange lingua gloriósi | Sing, my tongue, the mystery
Córporis mystérium, | of the glorious Body,
Sanguinísque pretiósi, | and of the precious Blood,
Quem in mundi prétium | which, as the price of the world,
Fructus ventris generósi | the fruit of a noble womb,
Rex effúdit géntium. | the King of the nations, poured out.
Nobis datus, nobis natus | Given to us, born for us
Ex intácta Vírgine, | of a spotless Virgin,
Et in mundo conversátus, | and having dwelt in the world,
Sparso verbi sémine, | the seed of the word once sown,
Sui moras incolátus | the days of His sojourn
Miro clausit órdine. | He closed in a wondrous way.
In suprémæ nocte cœnæ Recúmbens cum frátribus, | On the night of the Last Supper, at table with His brethren,
Observáta lege plene | the Law fully kept
Cibis in legálibus, | in the foods it prescribed,
Cibum turbæ duodénæ | as food to the band of Twelve
Se dat suis mánibus. | He gives Himself with His own hands.
Verbum caro, panem verum Verbo carnem éfficit: | The Word made flesh, by a word, makes true bread His flesh:
Fitque sanguis Christi merum, | and wine becomes the Blood of Christ;
Et si sensus déficit, | and if the senses fail,
Ad firmándum cor sincérum | to make a sincere heart firm
Sola fides súfficit. | faith alone suffices.
Tantum ergo Sacraméntum | So great a Sacrament, therefore,
Venerémur cérnui: | let us venerate, bowing low;
Et antíquum documéntum | and let the former teaching
Novo cedat rítui: | give way to the new rite;
Præstet fides suppleméntum | let faith provide its aid
Sénsuum deféctui. | for the failing of the senses.
Genitóri, Genitóque Laus et jubilátio, | To the Begetter and the Begotten be praise and jubilation,
Salus, honor, virtus quoque | salvation, honour, and might also,
Sit et benedíctio: | and blessing be to Them;
Procedénti ab utróque | to Him Who proceedeth from Both
Compar sit laudátio. | be equal praise.
Amen. | Amen.

Panem de cælo præstitísti eis. | Thou didst give them bread from heaven.
Omne delectaméntum in se habéntem. | Containing in itself all delight.
part 2
Orémus. | Let us pray.
Deus, qui nobis sub Sacraménto mirábili passiónis tuæ memóriam reliquísti: | O God, Who under a wonderful Sacrament hast left us a memorial of Thy Passion,
tríbue, quǽsumus, | grant us, we beseech Thee,
ita nos córporis et sánguinis tui sacra mystéria venerári; | so to venerate the sacred mysteries of Thy Body and Blood,
ut redemptiónis tuæ fructum in nobis júgiter sentiámus. | that we may continually feel within ourselves the fruit of Thy redemption.
Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. | Who livest and reignest world without end.
Amen. | Amen.

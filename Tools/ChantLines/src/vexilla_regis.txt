# Vexilla Regis prodeunt, all seven stanzas (the Passiontide text, Hoc
# Passionis tempore) and the Amen; the cantor breathes at the end of every
# verse line, so a line is a quarter of a stanza. Latin from the score;
# English plain and literal.

Vexílla Regis pródeunt: | The banners of the King go forth;
Fulget Crucis mystérium, | the mystery of the Cross shines out,
Qua vita mortem pértulit, | whereon Life endured death,
Et morte vitam prótulit. | and by death brought forth life.
Quæ vulneráta lánceæ | Wounded by the spear's
Mucróne diro, críminum | cruel point, that from the filth of our sins
Ut nos laváret sórdibus, | it might wash us,
Manávit unda et sánguine. | it poured forth water and blood.
Impléta sunt quæ cóncinit | Fulfilled are the things which David
David fidéli cármine, | sang in faithful verse,
Dicéndo natiónibus: | telling the nations:
Regnávit a ligno Deus. | God hath reigned from the Tree.
Arbor decóra et fúlgida, | O Tree, beautiful and shining,
Ornáta Regis púrpura, | adorned with the purple of the King,
Elécta digno stípite | chosen from a worthy stock
Tam sancta membra tángere. | to touch limbs so holy.
Beáta, cujus bráchiis | Blessed Tree, upon whose arms
Prétium pepéndit sǽculi: | hung the ransom of the world;
Statéra facta córporis, | it was made the balance of His Body,
Tulítque prædam tártari. | and took away the prey of hell.
O Crux ave, spes única, | Hail, O Cross, our only hope!
Hoc Passiónis témpore | In this time of the Passion
Piis adáuge grátiam, | increase grace in the devout,
Reísque dele crímina. | and blot out the crimes of the guilty.
Te fons salútis Trínitas, | Thee, O Trinity, fount of salvation,
Colláudet omnis spíritus: | let every spirit praise;
Quibus Crucis victóriam | to those to whom the victory of the Cross
Largíris, adde prǽmium. | Thou givest, add the reward.
Amen. | Amen.

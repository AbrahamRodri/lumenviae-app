Veni, veni, Emmánuel, | Come, come, Emmanuel,
Captívum solve Israël, | set captive Israel free,
Qui gemit in exsílio | who mourns in exile,
Privátus Dei Fílio. | deprived of the Son of God.
Gaude, gaude, Emmánuel | Rejoice, rejoice! Emmanuel
Nascétur pro te, Israël. | shall be born for thee, O Israel.
Veni, veni, Rex géntium, | Come, come, King of the nations,
Veni, Redémptor ómnium: | come, Redeemer of all,
Ut salves tuos fámulos | to save Thy servants,
Peccáti sibi cónscios. | who know their sin.
Gaude, gaude, Emmánuel | Rejoice, rejoice! Emmanuel
Nascétur pro te, Israël. | shall be born for thee, O Israel.

Veni, veni, O Oriens, | Come, come, O Dayspring,
Soláre nos advéniens: | console us by Thy coming;
Noctis depélle nébulas, | drive away the clouds of night,
Dirásque noctis ténebras. | and the dread darkness of night.
Gaude, gaude, Emmánuel | Rejoice, rejoice! Emmanuel
Nascétur pro te, Israël. | shall be born for thee, O Israel.
Veni, Clavis Davídica, | Come, Key of David,
Regna reclúde cǽlica; | open wide the heavenly kingdom;
Fac iter tutum súperum, | make safe the way on high,
Et claude vias ínferum. | and close the ways below.
Gaude, gaude, Emmánuel | Rejoice, rejoice! Emmanuel
Nascétur pro te, Israël. | shall be born for thee, O Israel.
Veni, O Jesse Vírgula: | Come, O Rod of Jesse:
Ex hostis tuos úngula, | from the claw of the enemy Thine own,
De specu tuos tártari | from the cave of hell Thine own
Educ, et antro bárathri. | lead forth, and from the pit of the abyss.
Gaude, gaude, Emmánuel | Rejoice, rejoice! Emmanuel
Nascétur pro te, Israël. | shall be born for thee, O Israel.

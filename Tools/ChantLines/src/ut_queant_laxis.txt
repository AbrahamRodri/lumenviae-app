Ut queant laxis resonáre fibris | That with loosened chords
Mira gestórum fámuli tuórum, | Thy servants may sound forth the wonders of Thy deeds,
Solve pollúti lábii reátum, Sancte Joánnes. | loose the guilt of our unclean lips, O holy John.
Núntius celso véniens Olýmpo, | A messenger coming from high heaven
Te patri magnum fore nascitúrum, | tells thy father thou shalt be born great,
Nomen, et vitæ sériem geréndæ | and sets forth in order thy name
Órdine promit. | and the course of thy life.
Ille promíssi dúbius supérni, | He, doubting the promise from on high,
Pérdidit promptæ módulos loquélæ: | lost the ready power of speech;
Sed reformásti génitus perémptæ | but thou, when born, didst restore
~
Órgana vocis. | the organs of his lost voice.
Ventris obstrúso récubans cubíli | Lying in the hidden chamber of the womb,
Sénseras Regem thálamo manéntem: | thou didst know the King abiding in His bridal chamber;
Hinc parens nati méritis utérque | whence each parent, by the merits of the child,
Ábdita pandit. | made known things hidden.
Sit decus Patri, genitǽque Proli, | Glory be to the Father and to the begotten Son,
Et tibi compar utriúsque virtus, | and to Thee, the Power equal to Both,
Spíritus semper, Deus unus, omni | O Spirit, ever one God,
Témporis ævo. | through all the ages of time.
Amen. | Amen.

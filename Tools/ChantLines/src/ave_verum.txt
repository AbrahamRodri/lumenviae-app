# Ave verum Corpus, mode VI, sung once through. Latin from the score;
# English from the Prayer Book (ave_verum). The cantor's shallow breaths
# after "Ave verum" and "immolátum" are not taken as line breaks.
noise -20
gap 0.2
merge 1.5

Ave verum Corpus natum | Hail, true Body, born
de María Vírgine: | of the Virgin Mary,
Vere passum, immolátum in cruce pro hómine: | that truly suffered and was sacrificed upon the cross for man;
Cujus latus perforátum | Whose side, when it was pierced,
fluxit aqua et sánguine: | flowed with water and with blood:
Esto nobis prægustátum | be for us a foretaste
mortis in exámine. | in the trial of death.
O Jesu dulcis! | O sweet Jesus,
O Jesu pie! | O loving Jesus,
O Jesu fili Maríæ. | O Jesus, Son of Mary.

# Jesu, dulcis memoria, its five stanzas and Amen. Latin from the score;
# English plain and literal (the app has no prayer for it). The breath
# before the last line of the first stanza is found only at a setting that
# cuts the soft ends of other lines, so its last two lines stand as one.

Jesu, dulcis memória, | Jesus, sweet remembrance,
Dans vera cordis gáudia: | giving true joys to the heart:
Sed super mel et ómnia Ejus dulcis præséntia. | but above honey and all things is His sweet presence.
Nil cánitur suávius, | Nothing more delightful is sung,
Nil audítur jucúndius, | nothing more pleasing is heard,
Nil cogitátur dúlcius, | nothing sweeter is thought upon,
Quam Jesus Dei Fílius. | than Jesus, the Son of God.
Jesu, spes pœniténtibus, | Jesus, hope of the penitent,
Quam pius es peténtibus! | how loving Thou art to those who ask!
Quam bonus te quæréntibus! | How good to those who seek Thee!
Sed quid inveniéntibus! | But what to those who find!
Nec lingua valet dícere, | No tongue can tell it,
Nec líttera exprímere: | nor any letter express it:
Expértus potest crédere, | one who has known it can believe
Quid sit Jesum dilígere. | what it is to love Jesus.
Sis, Jesu, nostrum gáudium, | Be Thou, Jesus, our joy,
Qui es futúrus prǽmium: | who art to be our reward:
Sit nostra in te glória, | may our glory be in Thee
Per cuncta semper sǽcula. | through all ages for ever.
Amen. | Amen.

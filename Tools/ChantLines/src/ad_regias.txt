Ad régias Agni dapes, | At the royal banquet of the Lamb,
Stolis amícti cándidis, | clothed in white robes,
Post tránsitum maris Rubri, Christo canámus Príncipi. | the Red Sea crossed, let us sing to Christ our Prince:
Divína cujus cáritas Sacrum propínat sánguinem, | whose divine charity gives us His sacred Blood to drink,
Almíque membra córporis Amor sacérdos ímmolat. | and Love, the priest, offers in sacrifice the members of His holy Body.
Sparsum cruórem póstibus | At the blood sprinkled on the doorposts
Vastátor horret Ángelus: | the destroying Angel shudders:
Fugítque divísum mare, Mergúntur hostes flúctibus. | the sea parts and flees; the enemies are drowned in the waves.
Jam Pascha nostrum Christus est, | Now Christ is our Pasch,
Paschális idem víctima: Et pura puris méntibus Sinceritátis ázyma. | and He the Paschal victim, and the pure unleavened bread of sincerity for pure minds.
O vera cæli víctima, Subjécta cui sunt tártara, | O true Victim from heaven, to whom hell is made subject,
Solúta mortis víncula, Recépta vitæ prǽmia. | the bonds of death are loosed, the rewards of life won back.
Victor subáctis ínferis Tropǽa Christus éxplicat, | Christ the victor, hell overthrown, displays His trophies,
Cælóque apérto, súbditum Regem tenebrárum trahit. | and, heaven opened, drags the king of darkness captive.
Ut sis perénne méntibus Paschále Jesu gáudium, | That Thou mayest be, O Jesus, an everlasting Paschal joy to our minds,
A morte dira críminum Vitæ renátos líbera. | free those reborn to life from the dread death of sin.
Deo Patri sit glória, Et Fílio qui a mórtuis | To God the Father be glory, and to the Son, who from the dead
Surréxit, ac Paráclito, In sempitérna sǽcula. | is risen, and to the Paraclete, for everlasting ages.
Amen. | Amen.

# Veni Sancte Spiritus, reple: the antiphon, its versicle Emitte Spiritum
# and the collect Deus qui corda. Latin from the scores; English from the
# Prayer Book (come_holy_ghost), the antiphon's last clause, which it does
# not print, plain.
noise -14
gap 0.3
merge 1.5

Veni Sancte Spíritus, | Come, Holy Ghost,
reple tuórum corda fidélium, | fill the hearts of Thy faithful,
et tui amóris in eis ignem accénde: | and kindle in them the fire of Thy love:
qui per diversitátem linguárum cunctárum, | who through the diversity of all tongues
gentes in unitáte fídei congregásti. | didst gather the nations in the unity of faith.
Emítte Spíritum tuum et creabúntur. | Send forth Thy Spirit, and they shall be created.
Et renovábis fáciem terræ. | And Thou shalt renew the face of the earth.
Orémus. | Let us pray.
Deus, qui corda fidélium Sancti Spíritus illustratióne docuísti: | O God, who didst instruct the hearts of the faithful by the light of the Holy Ghost,
da nobis in eódem Spíritu recta sápere, | grant us in the same Spirit to be truly wise,
et de ejus semper consolatióne gaudére. | and ever to rejoice in His consolation.
Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. | Through Christ our Lord.
Amen. | Amen.

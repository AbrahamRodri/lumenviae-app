# Media vita in morte sumus, the Lenten responsory (Mode IV): the
# response, its first verse, the response again from Sancte Deus, the
# second verse, Sancte again, the Gloria Patri, and Sancte a last time.
# The cantor breathes at the score's bars, and once inside adjutorem,
# where the line runs over both phrases. Latin from the score; English
# plain and literal.
noise -14
gap 0.3
merge 1.5

Média vita | In the midst of life
in morte sumus: | we are in death:
quem quǽrimus adjutórem, | whom shall we seek as our helper,
~
nisi te Dómine? | but Thee, O Lord,
qui pro peccátis nostris | who for our sins
juste irásceris: | art justly angered?
Sancte Deus, | Holy God,
Sancte fortis, | holy and mighty,
Sancte miséricors Salvátor, | holy and merciful Saviour,
amáræ morti | to bitter death
ne tradas nos. | deliver us not.
In te speravérunt | In Thee our fathers
patres nostri; | put their hope;
speravérunt, | they hoped,
et liberásti eos. | and Thou didst deliver them.
Sancte Deus, | Holy God,
Sancte fortis, | holy and mighty,
Sancte miséricors Salvátor, | holy and merciful Saviour,
amáræ morti | to bitter death
ne tradas nos. | deliver us not.
Ad te clamavérunt | To Thee our fathers
patres nostri; | cried out;
clamavérunt, | they cried,
et non sunt confúsi. | and were not put to shame.
Sancte Deus, | Holy God,
Sancte fortis, | holy and mighty,
Sancte miséricors Salvátor, | holy and merciful Saviour,
amáræ morti | to bitter death
ne tradas nos. | deliver us not.
Glória Patri, | Glory be to the Father,
et Fílio, | and to the Son,
et Spirítui Sancto. | and to the Holy Spirit.
Sancte Deus, | Holy God,
Sancte fortis, | holy and mighty,
Sancte miséricors Salvátor, | holy and merciful Saviour,
amáræ morti | to bitter death
ne tradas nos. | deliver us not.

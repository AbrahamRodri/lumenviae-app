# Litaniæ Sanctorum, the litany of the Rogation days as the Liber Usualis
# prints it, sung through as this recording has it: every Kyrie, Christe audi
# and exaudi twice; the saints from Sancta Maria to Omnes Sancti et Sanctæ
# Dei; Propitius esto twice; the eleven Ab and A, nine Per and In die judicii;
# Peccatores and the sixteen Ut to Fili Dei; the Agnus Dei; and the closing
# Christe and Kyrie once each. It ends there, without the Pater noster,
# psalm and collects that follow in the books. The app has no prayer for it:
# the Latin of the Kyrie, Pater de caelis, Propitius esto, Peccatores and
# Agnus Dei parts is the scores'; the invocations, which the scores do not
# print, are the Liber's: the count and order hold throughout, and Whisper
# heard nearly every one in its place (not Cosma et Damiane, Benedicte,
# Ut nos exaudire or Fili Dei, which stand where the count puts them).
# English plain. The cantor breathes inside the longer Ut petitions, and
# each half is its own line; in Ut inimicos Whisper heard too little of
# the first half to say where the breath fell, so it is one line (~).

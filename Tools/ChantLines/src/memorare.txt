# Memorare, set to chant (mode IV), whole; the score and the recording have
# no Amen. Latin from the score; English from the app's Memorare. The cantor
# breathes before "curro" and after "Noli", where the English cannot be
# parted without moving its words, so those two lines run over two phrases.
noise -20
gap 0.3
merge 1.5

Memoráre, | Remember,
o piíssima Virgo María, | O most gracious Virgin Mary,
non esse audítum a sǽculo | that never was it known
quemquam ad tua curréntem præsídia, | that anyone who fled to thy protection,
tua implorántem auxília, | implored thy help,
tua peténtem suffrágia, | or sought thine intercession,
esse derelíctum. | was left unaided.
Ego, tali animátus confidéntia. | Inspired by this confidence,
ad te, Virgo vírginum, mater, curro; | I fly unto thee, O Virgin of virgins, my Mother;
~
ad te vénio, coram te gemens peccátor assísto. | to thee do I come, before thee I stand, sinful and sorrowful.
Noli, Mater Verbi, verba mea despícere; | O Mother of the Word Incarnate, despise not my petitions,
~
sed audi propítia, et exáudi. | but in thy mercy hear and answer me.

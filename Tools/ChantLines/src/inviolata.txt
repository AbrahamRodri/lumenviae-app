Invioláta, íntegra, et casta es María: | Inviolate, spotless and chaste art thou, O Mary,
Quæ es effécta fúlgida cæli porta. | who art made the shining gate of heaven.
O Mater alma Christi caríssima: | O loving Mother of Christ, most dear,
Súscipe pia laudum præcónia. | receive our loving songs of praise.
Te nunc flágitant devóta corda et ora: | Devout hearts and lips now beseech thee
Nostra ut pura péctora sint et córpora. | that our hearts and bodies may be pure.
Tua per precáta dulcísona: | By thy sweet-sounding prayers
Nobis concédas véniam per sǽcula. | grant us pardon for ever.
O benígna! | O kindly one!
O Regína! | O Queen!
O María! | O Mary!
Quæ sola invioláta permansísti. | who alone hast remained inviolate.

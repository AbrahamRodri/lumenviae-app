# Flos Carmeli, the sequence: all eight stanzas, Amen and Alleluia. The
# cantor breathes at nearly every bar, so a line is a bar-to-bar piece; in
# the first stanza the first two run together. Amen and Alleluia stand as
# one line: Whisper hears the end of the Amen after the breath found between
# them, so they are not parted there. Latin from the score;
# English for the first two stanzas from the Prayer Book (flos_carmeli), the
# rest plain.
noise -14
gap 0.25
merge 1.5

Flos Carméli, vitis florígera, | Flower of Carmel, vine in blossom,
splendor cæli, | splendour of heaven,
Virgo puérpera | Virgin who didst bear a Son,
singuláris. | none like to thee.
Mater mitis, | Mother most gentle,
sed viri néscia, | who knewest not man,
Carmelítis | to thy Carmelites
esto propítia, | be thou gracious,
Stella maris. | O Star of the Sea.
Radix Jesse | Root of Jesse,
gérminans flósculum, | putting forth a little flower,
nos adésse | that we may be
tecum in sǽculum | with thee for ever
patiáris. | do thou permit.
Inter spinas | Thou who among thorns
quæ crescis lílium, | growest as a lily,
serva puras | keep pure
mentes fragílium, | the minds of the frail,
tuteláris. | as their guardian.
Armatúra | O armour
fortis pugnántium, | strong for them that fight,
furunt bella, | the wars rage:
tende præsídium | hold forth as a defence
Scapuláris. | the Scapular.
Per incérta | Through things uncertain
prudens consílium, | prudent counsel,
per advérsa | through adversity
juge solátium | unfailing comfort
largiáris. | do thou bestow.
Mater dulcis | Sweet Mother,
Carméli dómina, | Lady of Carmel,
plebem tuam | thy people
reple lætítia | fill with the gladness
qua beáris. | wherewith thou art blessed.
Paradísi | Of Paradise
clavis et jánua, | the key and the gate,
fac nos duci | cause us to be led
quo, Mater, glória | whither, O Mother, with glory
coronáris. | thou art crowned.
Amen. Allelúia. | Amen. Alleluia.

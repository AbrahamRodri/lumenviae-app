Sancte Míchaël archángele, defénde nos in prǽlio; | Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle.
contra nequítiam et insídias diáboli | Against the wickedness and snares of the devil
esto præsídium. | be our safeguard.
Ímperet illi Deus, | May God rebuke him,
súpplices deprecámur: | we humbly pray;
tuque princeps milítiæ cœléstis, | and do thou, O Prince of the heavenly hosts,
Sátanam aliósque spíritus malígnos, | Satan and all the evil spirits,
~
qui ad perditiónem animárum | who, seeking the ruin of souls,
pervagántur in mundo, | prowl about the world,
divína virtúte | by the power of God,
in inférnum detrúde. | cast into hell.

# Adoro te devote, all seven stanzas. Latin from the score; English from
# the Prayer Book (adoro_te).
noise -18
gap 0.3
merge 1.5

Adóro te devóte, latens Déitas, | I adore Thee devoutly, O hidden Deity,
Quæ sub his figúris vere látitas: | Who under these figures art truly concealed;
Tibi se cor meum totum súbjicit, | to Thee my whole heart subjects itself,
Quia te contémplans totum déficit. | for in contemplating Thee it wholly fails.
Visus, tactus, gustus in te fállitur, | Sight, touch and taste are deceived in Thee,
Sed audítu solo tuto créditur: | but by hearing alone is it safely believed;
Credo quidquid dixit Dei Fílius: | I believe whatever the Son of God has said:
Nil hoc verbo veritátis vérius. | nothing is truer than this word of Truth.
In Cruce latébat sola Déitas, | On the Cross the Godhead alone lay hidden,
At hic latet simul et humánitas: | but here the manhood too lies hidden with it;
Ambo tamen credens atque cónfitens, | yet believing and confessing both,
Peto quod petívit latro pǽnitens. | I ask for what the penitent thief asked.
Plagas, sicut Thomas, non intúeor: | I do not look upon the wounds, as Thomas did;
Deum tamen meum te confíteor: | yet I confess Thee to be my God;
Fac me tibi semper magis crédere, | make me ever more and more believe in Thee,
In te spem habére, te dilígere. | have hope in Thee, and love Thee.
O memoriále mortis Dómini, | O memorial of the death of the Lord,
Panis vivus vitam præstans hómini: | living Bread that gives life to man,
Præsta meæ menti de te vívere, | grant to my mind to live on Thee,
Et te illi semper dulce sápere. | and that Thou be ever sweet to its taste.
Pie pellicáne, Jesu Dómine, | O loving Pelican, Lord Jesus,
Me immúndum munda tuo Sánguine: | cleanse me, who am unclean, with Thy Blood,
Cujus una stilla salvum fácere | a single drop of which can make safe
Totum mundum quit ab omni scélere. | the whole world from every sin.
Jesu, quem velátum nunc aspício, | Jesus, Whom now I look upon veiled,
Oro fiat illud quod tam sítio: | I pray that what I so thirst for may come to pass:
Ut te reveláta cernens fácie, | that, seeing Thee with Thy face unveiled,
Visu sim beátus tuæ glóriæ. | I may be blessed in beholding Thy glory.
Amen. | Amen.

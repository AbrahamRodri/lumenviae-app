Regína cæli lætáre, allelúia: | O Queen of heaven, rejoice, alleluia.
Quia quem meruísti portáre, allelúia: | For He whom thou didst merit to bear, alleluia,
Resurréxit, sicut dixit, allelúia: | hath risen, as He said, alleluia.
Ora pro nobis Deum, allelúia. | Pray for us to God, alleluia.
Gaude et lætáre, Virgo María, allelúia. | Rejoice and be glad, O Virgin Mary, alleluia.
Quia surréxit Dóminus vere, allelúia. | For the Lord is truly risen, alleluia.
Orémus. | Let us pray.
Deus, qui per resurrectiónem Fílii tui Dómini nostri Jesu Christi mundum lætificáre dignátus es: | O God, who by the Resurrection of Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, didst vouchsafe to give joy to the world;

ut per ejus Genitrícem Vírginem Maríam | that through His Mother, the Virgin Mary,
perpétuæ capiámus gáudia vitæ. | we may obtain the joys of everlasting life.

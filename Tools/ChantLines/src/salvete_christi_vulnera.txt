# Salvete Christi vulnera (Mode II), the hymn of the Precious Blood, all
# nine stanzas and the Amen. Latin from the score; English a plain,
# literal rendering. The cantor breathes at every quarter of a stanza, so
# a line is a quarter.
noise -16
gap 0.2
merge 2.5

Salvéte Christi vúlnera, | Hail, wounds of Christ,
Imménsi amóris pígnora, | pledges of boundless love,
Quibus perénnes rívuli | from which unending streams
Manant rubéntis sánguinis. | of crimson blood flow forth.
Nitóre stellas víncitis, | In brightness you surpass the stars,
Rosas odóre et bálsama, | in fragrance roses and balsam,
Prétio lapíllos Índicos, | in worth the gems of India,
Mellis favos dulcédine. | in sweetness the honeycomb.
Per vos patet gratíssimum | Through you is opened a most welcome
Nostris asýlum méntibus: | refuge for our minds;
Non huc furor minántium | here the fury of threatening
Unquam penétrat hóstium. | foes never enters.
Quot Jesus in prætório | How many scourges Jesus,
Flagélla nudus éxcipit! | stripped, receives in the praetorium!
Quot scissa pellis úndique | How many drops of blood
Stillat cruóris gúttulas! | His torn skin sheds on every side!
Frontem venústam, proh dolor! | His comely brow, alas,
Coróna pungit spínea, | a crown of thorns pierces;
Clavi retúsa cúspide | nails with blunted points
Pedes manúsque pérforant. | pierce through His feet and hands.
Postquam sed ille trádidit | But after He had given up
Amans volénsque spíritum, | His spirit, loving and willing,
Pectus ferítur láncea, | His breast is struck with a lance,
Geminúsque liquor éxsilit. | and a twofold stream springs forth.
Ut plena sit redémptio, | That redemption may be full,
Sub torculári stríngitur, | He is pressed beneath the winepress,
Suíque Jesus ímmemor | and Jesus, unmindful of Himself,
Sibi nil resérvat sánguinis. | keeps for Himself no drop of blood.
Veníte, quotquot críminum | Come, all whom the deadly
Funésta labes ínficit: | stain of sin defiles:
In hoc salútis bálneo | in this bath of salvation
Qui se lavat, mundábitur. | whoever washes shall be made clean.
Summi ad Paréntis déxteram | To Him who sits at the right hand
Sedénti habénda est grátia, | of the Father most high be thanks,
Qui nos redémit sánguine, | who redeemed us by His blood
Sanctóque firmat Spíritu. | and strengthens us by the Holy Spirit.
Amen. | Amen.

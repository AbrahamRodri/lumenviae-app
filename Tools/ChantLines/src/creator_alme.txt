Creátor alme síderum, Ætérna lux credéntium, | Gracious Creator of the stars, everlasting light of believers,
Jesu, Redémptor ómnium, Inténde votis súpplicum. | Jesus, Redeemer of all, give ear to the prayers of Thy suppliants.
Qui dǽmonis ne fráudibus Períret orbis, ímpetu | Thou who, lest the world should perish by the deceits of the devil, by the force
Amóris actus, lánguidi Mundi medéla factus es. | of love wast moved to become the healing of a languishing world.
Commúne qui mundi nefas Ut expiáres, ad crucem | Who, to atone for the common guilt of the world, to the Cross
E Vírginis sacrário Intácta prodis víctima. | from the Virgin's sanctuary camest forth, a spotless Victim.
Cujus potéstas glóriæ, Noménque cum primum sonat, | At whose glorious power, and at whose Name as soon as it sounds,
Et cǽlites et ínferi Treménte curvántur genu. | both those in heaven and those below bow with trembling knee.
Te deprecámur, últimæ Magnum diéi Júdicem, | We beseech Thee, the great Judge of the last day:
Armis supérnæ grátiæ Defénde nos ab hóstibus. | with the arms of heavenly grace defend us from our foes.
Virtus, honor, laus, glória Deo Patri cum Fílio, | Power, honour, praise and glory to God the Father with the Son,
Sancto simul Paráclito, In sæculórum sǽcula. | together with the Holy Paraclete, for ever and ever.
Amen. | Amen.

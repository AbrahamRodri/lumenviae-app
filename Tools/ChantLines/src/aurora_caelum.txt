# Aurora caelum purpurat, the Easter hymn at Lauds (Mode VIII), all six
# stanzas and the Amen. This cantor breathes at the middle of each stanza,
# so a line is half a stanza. Latin from the score; English plain and
# literal.

Auróra cælum púrpurat, Æther resúltat láudibus: | The dawn makes the heavens crimson, the sky rings with praises;
Mundus triúmphans júbilat, Horrens avérnus ínfremit: | the world exults in triumph, hell shudders and groans,
Rex ille dum fortíssimus, De mortis inférno specu, | while that most mighty King, out of death's deep cavern,
Patrum senátum líberum Edúcit ad vitæ jubar. | leads forth the company of the Fathers, set free, to the light of life.
Cujus sepúlcrum plúrimo Custóde signábat lapis, | He whose tomb a stone had sealed, with many a guard,
Victor triúmphat et suo Mortem sepúlcro fúnerat. | rises in triumph as Victor, and buries death in His own tomb.
Sat fúneri, sat lácrimis, Sat est datum dolóribus: | Enough of mourning, enough of tears, enough is given to sorrow;
Surréxit exstínctor necis, Clamat corúscans Ángelus. | He is risen who has slain death, cries the shining Angel.
Ut sis perénne méntibus Paschále Jesu gáudium, | That Thou mayest be, O Jesus, the everlasting Paschal joy of our souls,
A morte dira críminum Vitæ renátos líbera. | free those reborn to life from the dread death of sin.
Deo Patri sit glória, Et Fílio, qui a mórtuis | To God the Father be glory, and to the Son, who from the dead
Surréxit, ac Paráclito, In sempitérna sǽcula. | is risen, and to the Paraclete, for ever and ever.
Amen. | Amen.

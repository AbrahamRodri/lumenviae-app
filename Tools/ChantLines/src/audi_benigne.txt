Audi, benígne Cónditor, Nostras preces cum flétibus, | Hear, kind Creator, our prayers poured out with weeping
In hoc sacro jejúnio Fusas quadragenário. | in this holy fast of forty days.
Scrutátor alme córdium, | Kind Searcher of hearts,
Infírma tu scis vírium: | Thou knowest how weak our strength is:
Ad te revérsis éxhibe Remissiónis grátiam. | to those who have turned back to Thee show the grace of pardon.
Multum quidem peccávimus, | Much indeed have we sinned,
Sed parce confiténtibus: | but spare us who confess it:
Ad nóminis laudem tui, Confer medélam lánguidis. | for the praise of Thy name bring healing to the sick.
Concéde nostrum cónteri | Grant that our body
Corpus per abstinéntiam, Culpæ ut relínquant pábulum Jejúna corda críminum. | be subdued by abstinence, that our fasting hearts may forsake the food of sin.
Præsta beáta Trínitas, Concéde simplex Únitas: | Grant, O blessed Trinity, grant, O simple Unity,
Ut fructuósa sint tuis Jejuniórum múnera. | that the gifts of fasting may bear fruit for Thine own.
Amen. | Amen.

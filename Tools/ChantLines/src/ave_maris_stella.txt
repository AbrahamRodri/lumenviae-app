Ave maris stella, Dei Mater alma, | Hail, O Star of the ocean, God's own Mother blest,
atque semper Virgo, felix cæli porta. | ever sinless Virgin, gate of heavenly rest.
Sumens illud Ave Gabriélis ore, | Taking that sweet Ave, which from Gabriel came,
Funda nos in pace, Mutans Hevæ nomen. | peace confirm within us, changing Eve's name.
Solve vincla reis, Profer lumen cæcis | Break the sinners' fetters, make our blindness day,
Mala nostra pelle, Bona cuncta posce. | chase all evils from us, for all blessings pray.
Monstra te esse matrem: Sumat per te preces, | Show thyself a Mother, may the Word divine
Qui pro nobis natus, Tulit esse tuus. | born for us thine Infant hear our prayers through thine.
Virgo singuláris, Inter omnes mitis, | Virgin all excelling, mildest of the mild,
Nos culpis solútos, Mitis fac et castos. | free from guilt preserve us meek and undefiled.
Vitam præsta puram, Iter para tutum: | Keep our life all spotless, make our way secure
Ut vidéntes Jesum Semper collætémur. | till we find in Jesus, joy for evermore.
Sit laus Deo Patri, Summo Christo decus, | Praise to God the Father, honor to the Son,
Spirítui Sancto, Tribus honor unus. | in the Holy Spirit, be the glory one.
Amen. | Amen.

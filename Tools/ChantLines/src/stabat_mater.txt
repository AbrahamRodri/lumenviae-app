Stabat Mater dolorósa | The Mother stood in sorrow,
Juxta crucem lacrimósa, | weeping beside the Cross
Dum pendébat Fílius. | whereon her Son was hanging.
Cujus ánimam geméntem, | Through her soul, lamenting,
Contristátam et doléntem, | saddened and grieving,
Pertransívit gládius. | the sword hath passed.
~
O quam tristis et afflícta | O how sad and how afflicted
Fuit illa benedícta | was that blessed one,
Mater Unigéniti! | the Mother of the Only-begotten.
Quæ mærébat et dolébat, | She mourned and she grieved,
Pia Mater, dum vidébat | the loving Mother, as she beheld
Nati pœnas ínclyti. | the torments of her glorious Son.
Quis est homo qui non fleret, | Who is the man that would not weep
Matrem Christi si vidéret | if he should see the Mother of Christ
In tanto supplício? | in so great anguish?
Quis non posset contristári, | Who could keep from sorrow,
Christi Matrem contemplári | beholding the Mother of Christ
Doléntem cum Fílio? | grieving with her Son?
Pro peccátis suæ gentis | For the sins of His own people
Vidit Jesum in torméntis, | she saw Jesus in torments,
Et flagéllis súbditum. | and given over to the scourge.
Vidit suum dulcem natum | She saw her own sweet Son
Moriéndo desolátum, | forsaken in His dying,
Dum emísit spíritum. | as He gave up His spirit.
Eia Mater, fons amóris, | O Mother, fount of love,
Me sentíre vim dolóris | let me feel the force of thy sorrow,
Fac, ut tecum lúgeam. | make me mourn with thee.
Fac ut árdeat cor meum | Make my heart to burn
In amándo Christum Deum, | in loving Christ my God,
Ut sibi compláceam. | that I may be pleasing to Him.
Sancta Mater, istud agas, | Holy Mother, do this for me:
Crucifíxi fige plagas | fix the wounds of the Crucified
Cordi meo válide. | deep within my heart.
Tui nati vulneráti, | Of thy wounded Son,
Tam dignáti pro me pati, | Who deigned to suffer so much for me,
Pœnas mecum dívide. | share with me the pains.
Fac me tecum pie flere, | Let me weep with thee in love,
Crucifíxo condolére, | and grieve with the Crucified,
Donec ego víxero. | as long as I shall live.
Juxta crucem tecum stare, | To stand beside the Cross with thee,
Et me tibi sociáre | and to join myself to thee
In planctu desídero. | in lamenting, is my desire.
Virgo vírginum præclára, | Virgin, most glorious of virgins,
Mihi jam non sis amára: | be not bitter to me now;
Fac me tecum plángere. | let me lament with thee.
Fac ut portem Christi mortem, | Let me bear the death of Christ,
Passiónis fac consórtem, | make me a sharer in His Passion,
Et plagas recólere. | and keep His wounds in mind.
Fac me plagis vulnerári, | Let me be wounded with His wounds,
Fac me cruce inebriári, | let me be inebriated with the Cross
Et cruóre Fílii. | and with the Blood of thy Son.
Flammis ne urar succénsus, | Lest I burn, enkindled by the flames,
Per te, Virgo, sim defénsus | by thee, O Virgin, may I be defended
In die judícii. | in the day of judgement.
Christe, cum sit hinc exíre, | O Christ, when I must go from hence,
Da per Matrem me veníre | grant that through Thy Mother I may come
Ad palmam victóriæ. | to the palm of victory.
Quando corpus moriétur, | When my body dies,
Fac ut ánimæ donétur | grant that to my soul be given
Paradísi glória. | the glory of paradise.
Amen. Allelúia. | Amen. Alleluia.
~
